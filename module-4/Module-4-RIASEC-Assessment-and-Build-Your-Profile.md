# RIASEC Profile Summary Activity

This activity walks you through taking the RIASEC assessment, interpreting your results, and connecting them to everything you already know about yourself from Module 2. By the end, you will have a complete **RIASEC Profile Summary** in your GitHub portfolio.

---

## Step 1 — Take the RIASEC Assessment

Complete the free RIASEC assessment using one of the following recommended tools:

- **O*NET Interest Profiler (Recommended)**
  Visit: [www.mynextmove.org/explore/ip](https://www.mynextmove.org/explore/ip)
  This is the official U.S. Department of Labor version — free, reliable, and directly linked to career data.

- **CareerOneStop Interest Assessment**
  Visit: [www.careeronestop.org/toolkit/careers/interest-assessment.aspx](https://www.careeronestop.org/toolkit/careers/interest-assessment.aspx)

Take the full assessment. Answer based on genuine interest, not perceived ability or what sounds impressive.

## Step 3 — Interpret Each of Your Top Three Types

For each of your top three RIASEC types, respond to the following prompts:

### My Top Three Types — Detailed Interpretation

#### Type 1: [I] — [Investigative]

**What this type typically values:**
[The Investigative type values problem solving, analyzing information, asking questions, and understanding how things work. People with this type normally enjoy having a problem in front of them that requires thought instead of simply being told exactly what to do.]

**How this shows up in my life:**
*(Think about your hobbies, favorite school subjects, activities you lose track of time doing, volunteer work, or part-time jobs.)*
[This result makes the most sense to me because I have always liked figuring out how technology works and finding the cause of a problem. When something is not working correctly, I usually want to understand why instead of just finding a quick way around it. This is also one of the reasons cybersecurity interests me. A lot of cybersecurity involves investigating suspicious activity, finding vulnerabilities, and understanding how someone could take advantage of a system.]

**Careers associated with this type that interest me:**
*(List at least 3 from the O*NET results or your own research.)*
1. *Information Security Analyst*
2. *Penetration Tester*
3. *Digital Forensics Analyst*

#### Type 2: [E] — [Enterprising]

**What this type typically values:**
[The Enterprising type values confidence, leadership, decision making, responsibility, and taking initiative. People with this type can enjoy being in positions where they are trusted to make decisions or help guide other people.]

**How this shows up in my life:**
[This was one of the more surprising results for me because I never really thought of myself as someone who was especially interested in management. After thinking about it more, I can see how it fits. I am confident in what I believe I am capable of, and I eventually want to become someone who has enough knowledge and experience that other people can rely on me. I would like to eventually take on more responsibility instead of staying in the exact same position throughout my career.]

**Careers associated with this type that interest me:**
1. *Cybersecurity Manager*
2. *Information Technology Manager*
3. *Cybersecurity Consultant*

---

#### Type 3: [R] — [Realistic]

**What this type typically values:**
[The Realistic type values practical work, hands-on experience, technology, and solving problems by actually working with something. People with this type normally prefer being able to see the results of what they are doing instead of only learning about something through theory.]

**How this shows up in my life:**
[I think this fits the way I learn. I understand something much better when I can actually use it instead of only reading about it. This has been true with programming and cybersecurity because being able to work through a problem myself makes it easier for me to understand what is happening. I also like technology in general and enjoy learning more about computers, programs, and how different systems operate.]

**Careers associated with this type that interest me:**
1. *Security Engineer*
2. *Network Security Engineer*
3. *Systems Administrator*
