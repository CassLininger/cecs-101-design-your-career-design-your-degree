### Career 2

**Career Title:**
[Penetration Tester]

**Brief Description** *(what does this person do day to day?):*
[A Penetration Tester looks for weaknesses in computer systems by attacking them in an authorized and controlled way. They may scan networks, test websites and applications, attempt to exploit vulnerabilities, document what they were able to access, and then explain how the organization could fix the problems they found.]

**Typical Education Required:**
[A bachelor's degree is a common route, although there are other ways into the field through certifications and experience. O*NET places Penetration Testers in a category that usually requires considerable preparation and technical experience.]

**Key Skills Required** *(list at least 6):*
1. *Critical thinking1*
2. *Programming and scripting*
3. *Network security*
4. *Vulnerability assessment*
5. *Complex problem solving*
6. *Systems analysis*

**Key Knowledge Areas Required:**
[Networking, operating systems, programming, web applications, computer hardware and software, telecommunications, vulnerability testing, and cybersecurity tools. Current job postings frequently mention Python, Linux, PowerShell, Nessus, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Nmap, AWS, Azure, and Kali Linux.]

**Median Annual Salary:**
[$116,580]

**Job Growth Outlook** *(growing, stable, declining?):*
[8% growth]

**Typical Career Path** *(how do people enter and advance in this field?):*

With more experience, a Penetration Tester could move into positions such as Senior Penetration Tester, Red Team Operator, Security Consultant, or Security Engineer.]
