# Step 6 — Reflect

**My Reflection:**  
The hardest part of making this plan was figuring out how all of my previous classes will actually fit into the Applied Cybersecurity degree at UTK. Since I transferred after completing my associate degree, my path does not look exactly like a student who started at UTK as a freshman. I know I have a lot of credits completed already, but I still need to make sure every one of them is being used in the best place possible. Another thing I noticed is that the cybersecurity degree gives me a lot more freedom with electives than I originally expected. I actually like that because I can use those classes to work on programming, cloud computing, web development, and other skills that relate to penetration testing. This plan also connects pretty well to my goal of getting more hands-on experience instead of relying only on what I learn from textbooks. I want to graduate knowing that I have actually used cybersecurity tools and worked through real problems before starting a full-time job. One thing I would like to improve is getting an internship before my last year instead of waiting until I am close to graduation. When I meet with my advisor, I mainly want to find out exactly which of my Columbia State credits still satisfy requirements and which classes I actually have left. I also want to ask which electives would give me the strongest technical background if I decide that penetration testing is the direction I want to pursue.
