# Step 6 — Reflect

**My Reflection:**  
This was more challenging than I expected because instead of only thinking about my courses semester by semester, I had to think about how my classes, career goals, and other plans would all fit together. One of the harder parts of creating the four-year degree plan was balancing my workload and figuring out where some of the required courses should fall. I also had to think about how to structure my electives, certifications, and internship opportunities so they would be the most useful for my goal of working in cybersecurity. Because I have already earned an associate’s degree in Information Systems, it was also important to make sure the credits I already have are being used as effectively as possible. My plan connects to my POV Statement because I know I want to work in the security field, but I also know I need to stay consistent in order to reach that goal. It also connects to my SMART Goals because I want to improve my work habits, gain internship experience, and work toward certifications outside of my normal coursework. Having everything planned out makes those goals feel more realistic because I can actually see where they fit into my future. I also know I need to make room for an internship so I can gain practical experience before graduating. When I meet with my academic advisor, I want to ask if the order of my classes makes sense and which electives would be most useful for an Information Security Analyst career. I also want to ask which certifications and internship opportunities would be best to work toward while I am still completing my degree
